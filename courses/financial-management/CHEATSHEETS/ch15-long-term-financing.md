| Feature | 의미 |
|--|--|
| Voting | Director election |

## §2 Voting

**Cumulative**: $\frac{S \times D}{N+1} + 1$ — minority protection.
**Straight**: Majority controls all.

## §22 1-line summary

> **Long-term financing = common + preferred + long-term debt. *Common* = voting + residual + limited. *Preferred* = fixed + priority + 50% tax exclusion. *Long-term debt* = bonds (indenture, covenants). *Internal dominant* (70-80%). *Pecking order* (Myers-Majluf). *Spread* 1-7%. *Country differences*. *Modern* — PE, SBC, ESG, crowdfunding.**
