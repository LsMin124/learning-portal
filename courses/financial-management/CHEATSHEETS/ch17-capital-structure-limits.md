## §3 Agency Costs of Debt

**1. Debt Overhang** (Myers 1977): NPV → bondholders first → equity refuses.

**2. Asset Substitution**: Equity = option, prefers volatility.

**3. Wealth Transfer**: Asset sale, surprise dividend, LBO.

## §4 Bond Covenants

**Negative**: D/E limit, dividend, asset sale, M&A.
**Positive**: Reporting, ratios.

## §5 Agency Costs of Equity (Jensen 1986)

> FCF → Empire building.

**Debt as discipline**: Forces distribution, reduces FCF.

## §6 Trade-off Theory

$$V_L = V_U + PV(TS) - PV(Distress) - PV(Agency)$$

**Optimal**: Marginal tax = Marginal distress + agency.

## §7 Pecking Order (Myers-Majluf 1984)

1. Internal funds
2. Debt
3. Equity (last)

**Info asymmetry**: Equity issue signal, stock falls ~3%.

## §8 Trade-off vs Pecking Order

| | Trade-off | Pecking |
|--|--|--|
| Optimal D/E | Yes | Path-dependent |
| Profit + debt | Positive | Negative |
| Fit | Cross-sectional | Time-series |

## §9 Miller (1977) Personal Tax

$$Net = 1 - \frac{(1-T_c)(1-T_E)}{(1-T_B)}$$

**Reality (US 2024)**: Net ~0%.

## §21 1-line summary

Direct 3-5%, indirect 10-25%. *Debt overhang + asset substitution + FCF (Jensen)*. *Pecking Order* (Myers-Majluf): info asymmetry. *Miller* personal tax reduces shield. *Industry + firm + cycle* specific. *Modern* — SBC, hybrids, leases, ESG, behavioral.**
